Senda is a free web browser for Android built on Mozilla's GeckoView engine, independent of Chromium. The interface is available in 8 languages: Spanish, English, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Chinese.

<b>Beta version.</b> It has been tested on a single phone model and has not had an external security audit. It is not yet recommended for sensitive data. Bug reports are welcome.

<b>Privacy and security</b>
<ul>
<li>Strict tracking protection, isolated third-party cookies, tracking-parameter stripping and Global Privacy Control.</li>
<li>Built-in uBlock Origin.</li>
<li>HTTPS-only mode and DNS over HTTPS, both on by default.</li>
<li>Built-in Tor network (optional), or your own SOCKS5/HTTP proxy.</li>
<li>Password vault encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with the key kept in the phone's secure hardware and unlocked with fingerprint or PIN. Import and export passwords (CSV from Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox, Bitwarden or KeePassXC).</li>
<li>Encrypted backup of bookmarks, settings, history and passwords with a passphrase only you know, in the open age format: save it wherever you want and open it with age on any system.</li>
<li>Optional app lock and screenshot protection. No telemetry.</li>
</ul>

<b>Browsing</b>
<ul>
<li>Separate normal and private tabs, bookmarks, history and downloads.</li>
<li>Reader mode and on-device page translation (the text never leaves the phone).</li>
<li>Print or save as PDF, find in page, desktop site, home screen shortcuts.</li>
<li>Home page with shortcuts and 64 freely licensed wallpapers that fill any screen; five are included and the rest are downloaded only when you choose them.</li>
</ul>

<b>Other</b>
<ul>
<li>Casting to a TV through Android screen mirroring (Miracast).</li>
<li>Optional assistant using your ChatGPT plan or your own API key (Claude, Gemini, Grok or Mistral). These are non-free network services: nothing is sent unless you use the assistant, and only to the service you choose. The conversation is kept encrypted on the phone behind fingerprint or PIN.</li>
</ul>

<b>Connections Senda makes on its own</b>
<ul>
<li>Google Safe Browsing, on by default (can be turned off in Settings).</li>
<li>uBlock Origin filter list updates.</li>
<li>Home page news from the RSS feeds of MuyLinux, EFF and FSF, only in layouts that show news.</li>
<li>Translation models from Mozilla, the first time a language is translated.</li>
<li>Wallpapers that are not included, only when you choose one, from the project's release on GitHub (GitLab as a fallback), with their SHA-256 checked.</li>
</ul>
